# Announcing Power BI reports, AI agents, and automations

A launch email should persuade users to try something because they understand its practical purpose, not because the technology sounds impressive.

## Decide the state of the launch

- **Live and accessible:** Announce as available only when confirmed; give the actual link or `[Link]` placeholder.
- **Pilot / limited access:** State clearly that it is a pilot and who can access it, if known.
- **Coming soon:** Avoid "now live" and avoid implying access is already enabled.
- **Feature change / enhancement:** Say what changed and what users should do differently, if anything.

## Structure

1. **Opening:** "We've launched [Tool] to help [Audience] [specific task]."
2. **Value:** Two or three verified, user-facing things it does. Avoid internal architecture details unless the recipients are technical.
3. **Action:** Where to access it and one clear thing to try, or the training/rollout step if not yet live.
4. **Limitations:** Add only important, grounded limitations: who has access, expected delays, required review of AI answers, or what the automation does not do.
5. **Closing:** Invite practical feedback or questions. No hype.

Use a short list for distinct capabilities; a table is usually unnecessary for one tool.

## Match the audience

- **Supervisors / operations:** Show a real work task and an example of what they can ask or check. Avoid engineering vocabulary.
- **Managers:** Explain who benefits, why it matters, and what participation you need.
- **Executives:** Lead with operational purpose, rollout and adoption picture, and a needed decision, if any.
- **IT / technical partners:** Mention integration, source, permissions, or support responsibility *only* when verified and relevant.

## Match the technology

- **Power BI:** Point to the decision or workflow the report supports and where it lives. Do not claim real-time data unless verified.
- **AI agent:** Give one or two realistic sample questions. Do not promise correct answers or autonomous actions beyond verified capabilities.
- **Automation:** Specify the trigger, action, and required user behavior when known. Do not imply an approval workflow if valid submissions activate automatically.

## Example: tool available

**Subject:** Introducing [Tool Name] | [Practical Purpose]

Hi Team,

We've launched [Tool Name] to help [audience] [specific task or problem].

With this tool, you can:
- [Verified capability 1]
- [Verified capability 2]

You can access it here: [Link]. To get started, try [simple first step].

Give it a try when you have a chance, and let us know if you run into any issues or have suggestions.

Thanks,  
[Your Name]

## Words to remove

"We're thrilled to unveil," "revolutionary," "transform the way we work," "unlock AI-powered insights," "seamless experience," and any fabricated time-saving or accuracy claim.
