# Project updates for managers and executives

Use for a consolidated status email covering several initiatives. The goal is to let a busy manager see what's moving, what's next, and whether anything needs attention. Don't turn a project update into self-promotion or a process essay.

## Decision rules

1. **Several projects:** Default to a table with `Project / Initiative | Status | Current Updates | Next Steps`.
2. **One project:** Prefer two or three short paragraphs, or bullets only if there are several distinct changes.
3. **Manager:** Show tangible progress and next steps. If known, make blockers or decisions easy to spot.
4. **Executive:** Lead with the important change, outcome, or decision needed. Shorten low-level detail; use a table only when it speeds comprehension.
5. **Unknown status or milestone:** Use a visible placeholder or omit the column if it would be mostly empty. Do not label work "In Progress," "Completed," or "On Track" without basis.
6. **Multiple dependencies:** Include a small `Support Needed` column only when a real decision, risk, or blocker is provided.
7. **Closing:** Always add a natural closing sentence *after* the table; do not stop at the last row.

## Approved voice

**Short opener:**

> I wanted to share a quick update on the projects I'm currently working on, including where things stand and what's coming up next.

**Preferred manager closing (use when it fits, not automatically):**

> I'll continue working through these items and share updates as we make progress. Happy to discuss any of them in more detail.

Use a different closing when the message needs a specific decision, review, or reprioritization. Avoid "advancing initiatives," "delivering measurable operational value," and "prioritizing high-impact opportunities."

## Copy pattern

**Subject:** Project Updates | Current Progress & Next Steps

Hi [Manager],

I wanted to share a quick update on the projects I'm currently working on, including where things stand and what's coming up next.

| Project / Initiative | Status | Current Updates | Next Steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Project A] | [Confirmed status] | [Work completed or underway] | [Known next action] |
| [Project B] | [Confirmed status] | [Work completed or underway] | [Known next action] |

I'll continue working through these items and share updates as we make progress. Happy to discuss any of them in more detail.

Thanks,  
[Your Name]

## Quality traps

- Do not pad every row with generic "working on improvements" text. If input lacks detail, use a specific placeholder.
- Do not claim launch, approval, delivery, or business impact unless verified.
- Do not add a gratuitous table if the user's email has only one item.
- Avoid redundant closing paragraphs that restate the table.
